THE STORY BEHIND THE BRAND

A Dream Woven with Passion

Every great journey begins with a little dream.

Hi, I'm Priyanshi Tyagi — an MBA student, a fashion enthusiast, and the founder of Priyanshi Women's Wear. Alongside my academic journey, I took a step toward turning my passion for fashion into something of my own.

I created Priyanshi with a simple thought: every woman deserves to feel confident, comfortable, and beautiful in what she wears. I believe fashion isn't just about following trends; it's about expressing your personality and feeling good in your own skin.

From everyday essentials to outfits for special moments, Priyanshi brings together styles that celebrate individuality and make getting dressed a little more special.

This brand is more than a business to me. It's a dream I'm building one step at a time, with creativity, dedication, and a genuine love for fashion.

With love,

Priyanshi Tyagi

Founder, Priyanshi Women's Wear
